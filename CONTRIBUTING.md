# Contributing

- **A place or a community is wrong or missing:** fix it on [BTC Map](https://btcmap.org); the atlas follows at the next refresh.
- **Events:** see "Add your events" in the README, or open the issue form "Add our events".
- **Ecosystem:** use the issue form "Ecosystem", always with a source.
- **Code:** a focused branch, `npm test` and `npm run build` passing, and a short explanation of the user-facing change. Keep pages free of trackers and of scripts from other sites, and keep the attributions.

Data changes are reviewed by hand; nothing is listed without a source.
